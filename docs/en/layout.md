# Layout

[Русский](../ru/layout.md) · **English**

The `layout` stage cuts a workbook into blocks with a period axis and body rows. Without fact rows a job can finish `succeeded` with an empty context. If there is no axis, the shape classifier builds a `params` block (label + value column) or drops prose and navigation. Those regions are not in the completeness denominator.

Code: `src/finance_context/layout/` (`detect.py`, `periods.py`, `params.py`) and reference parsing in `src/finance_context/formulas/engine.py`.

Related documents: [overview](overview.md), [graph](graph.md), [architecture](architecture.md).

## What counts as a block

A sheet collects **axis candidates**, then a body from one header to the next.

A calendar header is a row with ≥2 cells that `classify_header` treats as a date / year / quarter / month (`2024`, `2024E`, `Q1 2025`, `01.01.2026`).

Calendar spellings: years `2025`, `2025E`, `FY2025`, `FY25`, `FY 25`, `2025г.`, `2025 год`; quarters `Q1 2025`, `Q2'25`, `1Q25`, `2025 Q1`, `2025Q1`, `2025-Q1`, `2025/Q1`, `Q1-2025`, `Q1/2025`, `1 кв. 2025`, `I кв. 2025`, `1-й квартал 2025`, `1–й квартал 2025` (en dash), `Кв.1 2025` (key `2025Q1`); half-years `H1 2025`, `2H25`, `2025 H1`, `2025H2`, `2025-H1`, `Н1 2025`, `1Н25`, `2025 Н1`, `1 полугодие 2025`, `II полугодие 2025 г.`, `1 пол. 2025`, `1 п/г 2025`, `2П25`, `Полугодие 1` (key `2025H1`, grain `half_year`); months `янв.25`, `января 25`, `мая 25`, `марта 2025`, `августа 2025`, `сентябрь 2025`, `январём 2025`, `2025-01`, `01.2025`. Text left after exactly one date (`Факт 2025`, `2024 факт`, `2025 (план)`, `Budget 2026`, `Ожид. 2025`, `2031 ожид.`) stays on the header as residual text. The parser does not classify that text as fact or plan. A bare `Факт` or `План` with no date is not a period. Context schema `1.14.0` does not store an actual or plan label and adds no field for the marker. A suffix is written only when one axis repeats the same calendar key and the residuals differ: `<period_key>@<marker>`, the casefolded residual (`2024 факт` / `2024 план` → `2024@факт` / `2024@план`; `2024E` beside a bare `2024` → `2024@e`). A unique key stays plain whatever the header says. Equal residuals, including a repeated bare year, leave every key unchanged. Suffix letters on a year, quarter, half, month or date (`2025П`, `1 кв. 2025П`, `1 кв. 2025Ф`, `01.2025A`) stay letters. `П`, `P`, `Ф`, `A` and `E` are not fact or plan. Cyrillic lookalikes count only as that code: `Н` is the half marker `H` (`Н1 2025`, `1Н25`), and `2025Н` is not a year suffix. `FY25E` keeps the letter `e`; `FY2025` is the four-digit form. `Q5-2025`, `Q1/2`, `2025 Q`, `FY`, `FYI` and `FY250` are not periods. A month header is a real name, abbreviation or case form (`мая`, `янв.`, `сентябрь`), so `маяк 25`, `марка 25` and `июльский 25` are not May, March or July. `I кв.`, `H2`, `Н1` or `Полугодие 1` without a year composes with a year row above it. `2П` without a year does not. A case name (`Base`, `Upside`, `Базовый`) is not a period. A banner is the row above the date row. A merged cell covers its own span; unmerged text fills right until the next text. Text common to every column is dropped. A banner is used only when it has at least two groups and one of them separates duplicate dates. A banner over the whole axis is a title, a units row is metadata, and a year row that contains the periods under it is their parent, not a marker. The labels have to form one contiguous block each, or a pattern that repeats inside each duplicated date. A derived column (`SUM` of neighbours, `Ci-Cj`, `Ci/Cj` or `Ci/Cj-1`, or the same arithmetic in pasted values) is never a marker. A header with more than one date is a comparison and gets no period key. A 0/1 flag row, or a shift from constants to formulas, never creates a suffix: it only confirms a boundary when the tail and the banner disagree. An unresolved tie adds `Ambiguous period marker on {sheet}!r{row}: {keys}` to the existing warnings. Fill, borders and italics are not signals.

Format words live in `src/finance_context/layout/format_words.py`. Fact and plan are not among them. A word matches whole. A longer word matches only when the extra part is one grammatical ending (`кварталу` / `квартал`). Words of at most three letters stay exact, so `кв` is not `кВт` and `г` is not `график`. `кВт`, `квота`, `график`, `группа` and `годовой` are a closed list of surfaces that are not periods. A param header is the whole cell after punctuation becomes spaces (`Ед. изм.` is `ед изм`, `Ед. измерения` / `Единица изм.` / `Размерность` / `UoM` / `Unit of measure` are units, `Значение, руб.` and `Значение (руб.)` are the value column `значение руб`, `Источник` / `Описание` / `Source` / `Comment` / `Comments` / `Comment(s)` are notes, `Наименование показателя` / `Статья затрат` / `Параметры проекта` / `Исходные данные` are labels). When the whole caption is not in the list, the first word still carries its role if at most two short qualifiers follow (`Комментарии КП`, `Источник информации`, `Source of information`, `Note(s)`, `Выводы ПМ`). `Мероприятия` is a note. `Значение базовое` and `Value upside` are value columns; the header text stays as written. `Отрасль` and `Периодичность` are not notes. A total column is the sum of the period columns beside it: a `SUM` of that span, or cached values that equal the sum. `Итого`, `Итог`, `Всего`, `Total` and `Sum` are only a hint, so the word alone is not a period. One agreeing row is enough when the hint is present; without the hint the values have to agree on most rows. The same module holds stubs (`stub`, `частичный`), month names and every case form, the quarter and month axis labels, and `Start of period` / `End of period` (`начало периода`, `конец периода`). A numeric params column is a value column. `Base` and `Upside` keep that header text and are not classified. Label matching, a param-header lookup, and period codes fold `ё` to `е` and apply NFKC (`начало пёриода` matches `начало периода`, `Значениё` matches `значение`, `２０２５` is `2025`). Cyrillic/Latin lookalikes fold only inside a token that contains a digit: `Н1 2025` and `2025А` and `2025 A` parse as `H1 2025` and `2025A`, and a single Latin letter inside a Cyrillic month (`Mар.2025`) folds toward Cyrillic. `Ф` and `П` stay suffix letters on a date. A one-letter ordinal (`1-е полугодие`) is left as written. A column is `unit` when the cells under it are whole-cell unit tokens (the unit vocabulary): at least two cells and at least 60 percent of the column. A unit word inside a longer label does not vote. A column is `note` when the cells are free-form text of at least 24 characters, at least two cells and 60 percent of the column. Repeated identical prose is still `note`. Short text never reaches that rule. A column of numbers (at least three, and at least 60 percent) is `value` even when the header says unit or note. A mix below that share does not become `value`; the header word decides, and a short caption with no header word stays a value series when at least three numbers are present. The header word is a tie-break for an empty or sparse column. It does not override a clear content role, and it does not override `index`. The label column stays the left text column. A repeated ordinary word such as a category is not a measure, so it stays on the header hint or `unknown`. A cell that looks like a period (a year with a quarter/half word, an out-of-range `Q`/`H`, or a numeric `d.yyyy` such as `1.2025` or `13.2024`) and does not parse adds `Unparsed period header {sheet}!{addr}: «text»`. That warning does not invent a period.

A FAST date band (`Start of period` / `End of period`) is one axis of the sheet. The period key comes from the end date (the year for annual), and `header_row` is the end-date row (`PF Model!r7`, not the flag row). A column that has only an end date and no start (`Model_start` in L) has column kind `stub` and is not a period. 0/1 rows immediately under the ruler (`Construction` / `Operations`, formula `IF(AND(start>=G, end<=H),1,0)`) are not in the header band: they are flag rows of the block, and their 0/1 values attach to this axis as `flags`. A formula header that refers to the date row in the same column (`=YEAR(M7)`) is an alias of the same axis. Flag fingerprints are not compared for that alias.

A relative header (project finance) is a label `Year` / `Period` / `Month` / `Quarter` (and Russian год/период/мес/кв) with a contiguous integer run `0|1, 2, 3, …` of length ≥ 3 to the right. Period keys: `Y1` / `Q1` / `M1` / `P1`. Column kind: `relative`. Grain: `model_year` / `model_quarter` / `model_month` / `model_period`. `apply_grain` does not touch calendar keys.

After `build_context`, 0/1 flag rows attach to the axis whose rows contain them (`context.axes`). Each flag that is on writes `flags` on those periods. A repeated flag label does not overwrite the earlier row: its key is the normalized label plus the already-assigned key of the nearest different preceding flag in parentheses. A taken key keeps that form and adds the row number. With no preceding flag, the key is the label plus the row number. A calendar book without those flags gets `calendar_year`. Flag rows stay on the block with their `kind`.

If the row has no Year word, but there are ≥ 8 integers `0|1..n` to the right **and** those columns match the widest formula-copy run on the sheet (`formula_template` / R1C1), the axis is still built. A `Week #` counter under a calendar on the same columns does not become a second axis.

A candidate is dropped when it has fewer than two periods (calendar) or fewer than three (a labeled relative axis).

## Axis dominance

On one sheet, false headers often sit **left** of the real timeline: a `Start`/`End` date pair in assumptions next to 40 year columns on the right. A short calendar run **inside or to the right** of a wide axis is also false when the column set differs.

The widest candidate is `primary`. What remains:

- the same column set (±1 / Jaccard ≥ 0.8) — two tables stacked on one timeline;
- non-overlapping columns, including different width and grain (years on the left, months on the right), when each run has ≥3 periods (or ≥2 years) — two side-by-side bands;
- a narrow run of ≤2 periods left of a wide axis (Start/End) is still dropped;
- a header of ≥3 periods on another row stays its own block even when the columns overlap the wider axis. A run of ≤2 periods that overlaps that axis is still dropped.

Calendar columns of one header are split into runs at an empty visible column and at a grain change (`2020` vs `янв.25`). A gap that is only hidden columns stays one run, including when every period before the gap is hidden and the next header continues the sequence (`2023`, `2024`, an empty hidden column, `2025`). Hidden scratch that does not continue (`1965` beside `1939`) stays off that axis. A period that sits in a hidden column stays in that run and is published with `hidden`. A year repeated over the same columns as a finer axis does not become an axis: it writes `group_key` onto that axis's periods. A banner year that contradicts the year already inside the period key (`2014Q3` under `2008`) does not write `group_key`; a year row that matches the key does. A banner that contradicts every overlapping key is dropped. A year and a month in one header band with one label column are one table (`axis_ids`): rows once, value series per axis. The same column sequence and keys in the next section's header do not open a second axis: the section points at the first (`Output!r6c3`). Section blocks stay distinct. If two sections put different flags on the same period, the axes are not merged. Week and biweek axes are not collapsed when keys repeat.

The same `model_year` or `year` key sequence shares one period grid, even when the columns are shifted or the headers sit on different sheets. Each local axis stays in `axes` with its own id and columns and gets the fullest axis as `timeline_id`. `context.md` prints one table: a row per axis (the column letter under each period key) and the attribute rows (`Start`, `End`, `Group`, `Calendar`, `Flags`) under the same keys. A value stays in its own column: `Y1` can be column D on one sheet and E on another. Axes stay apart when both have dates and those dates disagree, or when two non-empty flag sets differ. `Y1` and `2024` do not merge. Different sequence lengths do not merge either.

`body_rows` are cut from the **filtered** list, not from the raw header bands. A label-only row whose period columns are empty (no number, text, or formula), with only empty rows between it and the next header, labels that next block, including when that row is hidden: the title stays and `hidden` is set. A row that still has a period value or a formula stays in the block above. A hidden row that has values joins that same block, with `hidden` set on the row. Row visibility comes from the workbook's `hidden_rows`. A visible row whose only text sits in a hidden column is not a hidden row, and that scratch text does not become a section title.

Formula-copy (the same `formula_template` in neighboring columns, length ≥ 3) sets the timeline geometry: headers confirm it. Flags and constants that are not copied along the row are not an axis.

## Label zone

Previously `label_col = min` of the text column: sections in col 2 and articles in col 4 made the body skip silently.

Now each body row takes the leftmost visible text cell left of the axis (not a number, not a period). The histogram of those “left” columns gives `block.label_col` (argmax; the left one wins a tie). A hidden scratch column does not vote, so it does not become `label_col`. `span` is those columns left of primary, plus primary. A hidden row is read in that same span, and its label cell keeps `hidden`.

A row label is the first non-empty cell of the span; `indent` is the position in the span, plus leading spaces, plus the label cell's alignment indent (`styles.xml` `cellXfs/xf/alignment@indent`). Italic text is not an indent. A row outline level (`sheetData/row@outlineLevel`) is read with `summaryBelow` (`sheetPr/outlinePr`, or `sheetFormatPr/outlinePr` when a producer puts the flag there). Level 1 under a level 0 band stays with the indent parent. A deeper level chooses the summary row inside that group when it sits closer than the indent parent: above the detail when `summaryBelow` is false, and below it when `summaryBelow` is true (Excel's default). A column `outlineLevel` is stored on the cell and does not change the row parent. A `LayoutRow` has its own `label_col` (the address in context) when the article is not in `block.label_col`. The word `Total` / `Sum` / `Итого` / `Всего` in the label column is the row name, not a column header; so are `План`, `Факт`, `Budget` and `Base case`. Only a period there (`2024`, `I кв. 2025`) is not a row name. If the span has no label, the first text left of the period columns is used (`CHECK`).

Row kinds (`fact` / `abstract` / `index` / `helper` / `flag`) are as in [architecture.md](architecture.md). `index` only when the axis runs `0|1, 2, 3, …` and the label is a counter (`week`, `#`, …) **or** the period cells have no formulas. The “every number ≤ 12” branch is gone: money 1..12 with formulas stays `fact`. Placeholders `Spare` / `None` are `helper`. Kind `flag` is only a binary series (≥90% of values in `{0, 1}`) or a `flag` token in the label. `Mid case` / `Low case` / `Applied (real terms)` with prices stay `fact`. `Case Number` is the `1..n` case-number row, not a data row. A selector (`Live Case`, `* choice`) is `flag`. A scalar left of the ruler (IRR, NPV, `Months per year`) with a numeric role cell and no period values is `fact`, not `abstract`. A row under a `Check` outline stays `helper`.

## Formulas and the axis

Compile marks a formula `unparsed` when a token is not parsed. A range with the sheet qualifier **on the right end** (`SUM(TBA!$D$10:'TBA'!D10)`) is allowed: the sheet prefix after `:` is skipped. Otherwise structure does not see the SUM/edges, and warnings accumulate in context.

Cached values are not required for layout when the header text is already a calendar or a year index.

## Blocks without a period axis

If the sheet has no axis, `detect_params_block` classifies the shape (not a whitelist of sheet names):

| Shape | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A label in the left text column **and** a value column (numbers / dates / short scalars) on ≥3 rows | `block.kind=params` |
| A 2-D grid whose row label is itself a number (`Sensitivity`) | Header row only, `kind=abstract` |
| Numbered rows under a header of ≥3 text captions (`№ п/п`, `Показатель`, a sentence that contains a date) | `params` table. The date stays inside the caption and is not a period key |
| Long prose, Cover, Disclaimer, shortcuts, including a hidden option list with no textual value header | Region dropped, not in completeness |

A params header may be multi-row: column captions and `Units` / `Values` / `Active` / `Notes` are collected per column. A numeric column is `value`, and the header text stays verbatim (`Base`, `Upside`, `Low`). Several value columns in one block take a slug of the header (`Base Case` → `base-case`, `Базовый` → `базовый`) so their observations stay apart. The slug is the casefolded header: Unicode letters stay as written, `ё` folds to `е`, nothing else is transliterated, and every other character is a separator. A slug with no letter is not a name, so the first such column keeps `value` and a repeated one becomes `value-<column>`. A repeated letter slug becomes `value-<column>` too. A single value column, and a header the closed list already calls a value (`Значение`, `Value`), keep the key `value`. A column of measure tokens is `unit`. A column of long text is `note`. A short non-numeric caption such as `Отрасль` is `unknown`. A caption that looks like a period but did not form an axis (`9М 2025`) stays `unknown` too. Context adds one line per block, `Unrecognized column headers in {block_id}: «Отрасль», «Периодичность»`. When two or more captions look like periods but formed no axis (`9М 2025`, `FY2025/26`, `Ожидаемое 2025`, `Estimate 2025`, `2025/26`, `Год 1`, or parsed periods split by one that does not parse), the line is `No time axis on {sheet}: headers {sheet}!B1:D1 look like periods but do not form an axis («9М 2024», …); columns kept as unknown`. `№ п/п`, `№`, `#`, and `No.` are role `index`, not `unknown`. A long caption, or a numeric column with no caption, stays `value`. A numeric cell is not a header. A label-and-amount list with no header row keeps every row, including the first; the amount column is `value` and its header text is empty. The role name is not written out as header text. A hidden row inside that table joins it with `hidden` set and keeps its value cells. A hidden column that is only an option list, with no visible table around it, does not become a scenario column and does not open a params block. The **active value** column is chosen by the number of cross-sheet dependents in `ir/edges.parquet`, and only when that column already has a textual caption; a header word is a hint. It does not relabel the other columns as scenarios.

A hidden **sheet**, **row**, and **column** are read like visible ones. `SheetLayout.hidden` comes from the workbook metadata. Parse also records `hidden_rows` and `hidden_cols` on each sheet. Context adds one line per fact, with consecutive addresses collapsed: `Hidden sheet {name} is included`, `Hidden rows {name}!17:18,20:22 are included`, `Hidden columns {name}!W:AA are included`. A hidden row or column is listed only when it holds a value or a formula; an empty hidden range, including unused columns through XFD, is silent. A published row, period, and role cell carry `hidden` when that row or column is hidden; the flag is omitted when it is false. `label_col` still follows the visible text column.

A scenario switch row is not a column header. It is a control (`kind=flag`). The row holds a constant cell that formulas on other rows pass to `INDEX`, `CHOOSE`, or `OFFSET`. A same-row `OFFSET`, or an `IF` that only mentions a driver, is not that switch. When two such cells exist, a label such as `Scenario Chosen` keeps its row. The live index (often `D3`) stays a block row; column D is the selected value, F:… is the case matrix. Numbers `1..n` on the switch row do not overwrite axis names (`Base Case`). The row does not vote for the `value` column, or `label_col` slides right.

An all-caps row with no values becomes `abstract`. A `Check` / `Result` table becomes `helper` / `check_row` and stays on the block.

On a sheet **with** an axis, `carve_params_regions` cuts out rows that have no values to the right of the case span:

| Region | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A run of `1..n` inside the period columns (n ≥ 3 and not wider than 60% of those columns) | `params`: a caption such as `Live Case` is `value`. The column name is the row label, with a trailing index word (`Number`, `No.`, `#`, `№`) left off, plus the column's place (`Case Number` → `Case 1`, key `case-1`; `Кейс №` → `Кейс 1`, key `кейс-1`). The number in the cell is not the name. The selector (`Live_case`, `OFFSET`) is a `flag` row |
| Checks and constants (Reference / Result / a scalar in E/G/H), when there are ≥ 3 fact/helper rows | a separate `params` block |

Those rows do not stay in the timeline block. The case columns are not part of the axis.

## Non-period cells on a timeline

Columns between the label zone and `min(period_col)` get roles on `LayoutRow.cells`: `SUM` over the period columns → `total`; unit text (`k£`, `EUR'000`, `RURm`, `RUR`, `mRUB`, `USD bn`, `RUB'000`, `EUR/MWh`, `%`, `x`, `years`, a whole cell `Index`, `MWh p.a.`, `#`, `№`) → `unit`. The label cell of the period-header row (`RURm` beside `2020`) is the unit of the block. A body row whose label is only such a caption (`% p.a.`, `k£`, `EUR'000`, `тыс. руб.`) covers the following rows until the next section. A header `Year` and a line item `Euro` are not that caption. A unit role cell on the row itself wins. A column title `Index` stays a header. `Base Index` is not a unit. A trailing `p.a.` is a period marker on the caption (`MWh p.a.` is count, `EUR p.a.` is money, `% p.a.` is rate); a column with only an end date and no start → `stub`; any other number/formula → `value`. A role cell has `header` — the nearest label in the same column inside the section (`Start`, `End`, `Live Case`, `Min`, `Avg`). `total` and `stub` are not periods of the axis; the graph link for that cell has `period_id=null`. These are scalars such as `Construction!C22`, not a dump of every cell on the sheet.

## What to check when the context has no fact rows

| Symptom | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| 0 blocks | No calendar axis, no `Year`+`1..n`, no long `1..n` on a formula-copy, and the shape is not params (prose / nav) |
| Many 2-period blocks, 0 fact | False Start/End pairs were not filtered, or labels sit to the right of `label_col` |
| Blocks exist, fact = 0 | Label zone / skip of an empty `label_col` |
| No `D3` / Scenario Chosen among block rows | The control row landed in the params header (`params.py`) |
| `Unparsed formula` on a header or a SUM | `formulas/engine.py` |

Public corpus: Packt (year index + `Input Assumptions` as params) and RVI (calendar + sections in several columns; `Top Shortcuts` produces no rows). The `three-statement` lock entry is marked unavailable: the cited GitHub tree never contained the workbook and stored no checksum. `scripts/fetch-corpus.py` skips it.

Detector tests: `tests/layout/`. On `cashflow.xlsx`, params blocks appear (Assumptions / Checks); Cover and Disclaimer still have no rows. Timeline `label_col` of the weekly blocks must not slide.
